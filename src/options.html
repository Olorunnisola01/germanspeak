How it works
Select (highlight) any text on a page. A small icon appears near your selection — click it to open a card with the original text, its translation, and Play buttons for both languages. GermanSpeak automatically detects the language: German text is translated to English, and anything else (e.g. English) is translated to German.
  • Microsoft Edge: Prefers the excellent online "Natural" neural voices (crystal clear cloud voices)
  • Google Chrome: Prefers Google voices + high quality local voices

Tips for best experience
  • Be online for the highest quality Edge "Online (Natural)" voices.
  • Select text → small icon appears → click it to translate & hear both languages.
  • Use Alt+Shift+T to translate the current selection without the icon.
  • Right-click a selection for "Translate selection" or "Speak selection".
Translation uses Google Translate (free public endpoint).
Speech uses the browser's built-in Web Speech API (no data leaves your device for TTS).
